UN Regulation / R141

Tire pressure monitoring.

A focused verification view for the tire pressure monitoring system within the M1 homologation program.

System intent

Monitor tire pressure continuously and provide the driver with a clear warning when pressure loss reaches the applicable threshold.

Verification focus

Confirm warning behavior, telltale visibility, pressure-loss detection, and system response across the defined vehicle conditions.

Evidence package

Record vehicle configuration, tire and wheel fitment, test conditions, measured pressures, warning timing, and final compliance status.
