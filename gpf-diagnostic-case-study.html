GPF Diagnostic Development

A system-level diagnostic decomposition that starts with the Gasoline Particulate Filter functions, identifies representative failure modes and effects, and connects them to OBD monitoring concepts and physical implementation elements.

04 / Systems engineering traceability

Turn failure analysis into testable requirements.

01Function

Define the intended GPF behavior: capture, store, and burn soot.

02Failure mode

Identify how each function can be lost, degraded, or behave outside the intended range.

03Effect

Connect the failure to emissions, restriction, regeneration, performance, or thermal consequences.

04Diagnostic monitor

Define the sensing, model, plausibility, threshold, and enabling-condition concept needed for detection.

05Allocation

Assign implementation responsibility to sensing, ECU software, controls, and calibration.

06Verification

Define software, system, and vehicle-level criteria that demonstrate the diagnostic requirement is satisfied.
