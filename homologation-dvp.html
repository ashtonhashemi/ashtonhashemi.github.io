Certification architecture / M1 vehicle

Homologation DVP

A requirements-to-evidence view for turning vehicle certification obligations into a controlled Design Verification Plan.

The premise

One vehicle. Three regulatory lanes.

Homologation work becomes manageable when every requirement has a clear owner, a verification method, and an evidence trail. This draft maps the M1 certification structure across United States, European Union, and UN regulatory sources before the detailed test matrix is built.
